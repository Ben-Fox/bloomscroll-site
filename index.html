Bloomscroll Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 15, 2026

Bloomscroll collects no data. None. Everything you do in the app stays on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. Bloomscroll has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party services. The app does not make network requests.

Where your garden lives

Your completed tasks and settings are stored only in the app's local storage on your phone. They are never transmitted anywhere. If your device performs system backups (Google or iCloud device backup), your garden may be included in your own backup under your control, like any other app's data.

Deleting your data

Uninstall the app. That is the entire process. Everything Bloomscroll ever knew about you goes with it.

Children

Since Bloomscroll collects nothing from anyone, it collects nothing from children either.

Changes

If a future version of Bloomscroll ever adds an online feature, this policy will be updated before that version ships, and the app will make any data use clear and optional.

Contact

Questions can be sent through the app's store listing contact options.

Bloomscroll: do one small real thing instead of scrolling. Your garden never leaves your phone.
